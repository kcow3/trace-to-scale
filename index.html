        Step 1 of 5

        Load a 3D scan

        Open a PLY, OBJ or STL file. Point clouds and meshes from LiDAR scanning apps both work.

        You can also drop a file onto the viewer.

        Scanning with an iPhone
        1. Use an iPhone or iPad Pro with LiDAR and a scanning app such as Scaniverse, Polycam or 3d Scanner App.
        2. Choose the small-object or detail mode. Put the part on a plain table with space around it.
        3. Move slowly all the way around, then scan from above as well.
        4. Export as PLY (point cloud) or as OBJ or STL (mesh), and send it to your computer with AirDrop or Files. You can also open this page on the phone itself.
