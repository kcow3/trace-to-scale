Trace to Scale Turn a photo into a to-scale Fusion 360 sketch
v1.2

    Step 1 of 7

    Load a photo

    Use a photo of the part with something of known size beside it, like a ruler, bank card or coin.

    You can also drop a photo onto the canvas, or paste one with Ctrl+V.

    Step 2 of 7

    Straighten the photoOptional

    Shot at an angle? Mark the corners of a flat rectangle of known size lying beside the part. This removes the perspective and sets the scale for you.

    Step 3 of 7

    Set the scale

    Draw a line across something you know the size of, then enter its real length. The longer the reference, the more accurate the result.

    Step 5 of 7

    Refine the shapeOptional

    Fix glitches by moving or deleting points, and smooth out jagged edges.

    Click a shape to select it, then drag any point to move it. Drag across empty space to select several points. Double-click a line to add a point. Right-drag or hold Space to pan.

    Step 6 of 7

    Fit for printingOptional

    Adjust the exported shape for your printer’s tolerances. The trace on the photo stays as it is.

    Scale%
    Outlinesmm
    Holesmm

    Positive values make a shape bigger. For a case or pocket the part must fit into, grow outlines by your printer’s clearance, often 0.2 to 0.4 mm. Holes are shapes that sit inside another outline.

    Step 7 of 7

    Export to Fusion 360

    In Fusion: Insert › Insert DXF, pick a plane, set units to mm. Geometry is placed relative to your origin.

    In Fusion: Insert › Canvas, then right-click the canvas › Calibrate and click the two ends of the scale bar.
